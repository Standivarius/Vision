PRIVACY DETAILS FOR ONLINE AI HELP
Standivarius Doxa Setup | 9 November 2026

WHO PROVIDES IT
Doxa Setup is provided by DMR Ergonomics S.R.L. (Standivarius), Calea Circumvalatiunii 37, Sc B, 6th floor, Apt 27, 300671 Timisoara, Romania. Privacy and support enquiries: office@standivarius.com.

OPTIONAL AND AUTOMATED
Online AI Help is an automated Doxa setup adviser, not a human technician. It can make mistakes. The standard Doxa installation, device checks and Offline help do not require Online AI Help. The online option is disabled until you choose it, and may also be blocked by your organisation. The AI cannot run commands, change Windows settings, read personal files or install drivers.

DATA SENT WHEN YOU ASK A QUESTION
For each online question, the client sends your typed question and selected language; Windows build; driver version, signature and installed-package indicators; Doxa hardware-detection state; the number of detected Doxa displays; active Windows-desktop count where available; local or remote session type; and limited fixed diagnostic flags. It also sends a pseudonymous installation identifier, the installation's PUBLIC RSA signing key, a request timestamp and a digital signature. The PRIVATE signing key remains protected in your Windows profile and is never sent. The identity is created automatically; no manual device approval or account registration is required.

The service necessarily receives network metadata, such as your IP address and request time. It applies per-installation, network and global usage limits to prevent abuse. The Doxa client does not intentionally send screenshots, passwords, complete USB identifiers, full system logs, Windows account names, installed-application lists or arbitrary files. Personal or confidential information you type yourself may still be included in a question: please do not enter it.

HOW THE SERVICE USES THE DATA
The question and limited diagnostic facts are sent over HTTPS to the Standivarius Doxa service hosted on Cloudflare Workers. Cloudflare Workers AI generates a troubleshooting answer using reviewed Doxa technical material. This is for answering your request and protecting service availability, not for controlling your computer or evaluating you as an employee. No marketing use is implemented in the Doxa AI request path.

STORAGE AND LIMITATIONS
The Doxa Worker code does not create a conversation-history database. Its configured global quota stores dates and numbers of AI calls, not the text of questions. Cloudflare and network infrastructure may process technical request logs under their own operating arrangements. Exact provider-log retention, processing regions and international transfer safeguards require confirmation before wider commercial rollout; no specific period or region is promised here.

YOUR CHOICES AND RIGHTS
You may keep Online AI Help off, switch to Offline help at any time, or close Setup. Disabling AI prevents new online questions but does not withdraw a question already transmitted. Your organisation may prohibit AI through Windows policy. For access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection or other data-protection queries, write to office@standivarius.com. You may also complain to your competent data-protection authority. The opt-in is a product permission; the applicable GDPR legal basis and any enterprise controller/processor roles require separate approval before general release.
