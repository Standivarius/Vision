## Source-truth comparison

Source inspection after the freeze confirmed:

- WGC/D3D11/DXGI architecture exactly.
- EvidenceSampler combines UIA and Win32 providers.
- UIA and Win32 candidates are filtered against the source rectangle before routing.
- View update flow is evidence snapshot -> movement permission -> ViewUpdateStep/ViewController -> renderer.
- source_priority gives deliberate pointer intent a short-lived priority, then UIA caret, Win32 caret, then UIA/Win32 focus; freshness and a pointer-intent barrier influence ownership.
- TopologyReconciler is explicitly constructed with 500 ms and rebuilds workspace generations in-process.
- settings.ini stores the reconstructed settings/role groups.
- ViewController saves the magnified zoom and viewport on entry to 1x and restores them on return.
- the Detail/context output windows use non-activating/topmost/tool-window and capture-exclusion mechanisms as reconstructed.

## REA/Ghidra performance

Observed on this Dell:

- binary overview: seconds
- full Ghidra search analysis: roughly 196 seconds
- trace from workspace_generation_ string to containing function: roughly 214 seconds
- targeted decompile of FUN_140025de0: roughly 300 seconds
- decompiled pseudocode: about 68k characters

The trace found workspace_generation_ at 0x1400479c8, referenced from code inside FUN_140025de0.

The decompile recovered material control-flow clues including:
- Context/Detail binding branches
- workspace_generation_
- SetWindowDisplayAffinity(..., 0x11)
- multi-source readiness/self-test strings
- settings/help lifecycle
- topology self-test signaling
- capture/topology shutdown branches

Therefore REA is useful for deep follow-up, but not as the first discovery tool on this host.

## Recommended Windows reconstruction stack

Use a funnel:

1. Fast discovery
   - PE imports/headers
   - Sysinternals Strings
   - ListDLLs / process tree
   - runtime window styles and module census
   - controlled black-box behavior

2. REA/Ghidra targeting
   - trace a meaningful string/API clue to xrefs
   - identify containing functions
   - decompile only the relevant functions
   - preserve evidence and confidence labels

3. Escalate only when needed
   - ProcMon for file/registry/process activity
   - ETW/WPR/WPA for timing and process/thread behavior
   - direct Ghidra for DLLs or artifacts outside REA Windows P0 admission
   - debugger/TTD or Frida only for unresolved runtime decision logic

## Applicability to ZoomText and SuperNova

This calibration is sufficient to adopt the method for targeted competitor mechanism investigations.

Do not attempt broad product cloning or exhaustive decompilation.

Use one Vision design question at a time, for example:
- what runtime evidence causes caret following?
- what mechanism prevents magnified output from feeding itself?
- how are monitor/source roles represented?
- what causes ownership transfer between source monitors?
- what hysteresis/debounce prevents oscillation?

Expected limitations relative to Vision:
- commercial binaries may expose fewer helpful strings/RTTI;
- important logic may live in DLLs, services or drivers;
- REA Windows P0 does not cover every PE role, although installed Ghidra and Sysinternals can inspect those components directly;
- physical multi-monitor questions still require the Dell with real external displays.

The calibration therefore proves the workflow, not that ZoomText/SuperNova will yield the same 75 percent coverage.
