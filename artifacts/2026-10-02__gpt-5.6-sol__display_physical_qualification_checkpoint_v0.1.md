## Bounded conclusions

### #14 topology reconciliation

**PASS for external HDMI disconnect/reconnect.**

For this path:
- WM_DISPLAYCHANGE did not terminate the product process;
- Vision rebuilt in-process;
- temporary missing Detail intent did not overwrite persistence;
- the correct Detail display was restored after reconnect;
- no wrong-monitor binding was observed;
- generation 3 rendered successfully after recovery.

The broader issue remains open until the planned physical protocol provides enough evidence for other supported topology mutations.

### #13 lifecycle/debounce

**500 ms is supported for this HDMI cable path.**

The immediate notification clusters around each WM_DISPLAYCHANGE settled inside ~300 ms. Later WM_SETTINGCHANGE/WM_DEVICECHANGE notifications occurred, including one >5 s later, but no later WM_DISPLAYCHANGE invalidated the rebuilt topology and Vision remained correct.

This suggests topology debounce should remain tied to actual topology-invalidating evidence rather than resetting on every later generic device/settings notification.

Broader lifecycle scenarios remain required before freezing a universal rule.

### #11 durable identity

**Positive evidence for same-port HDMI disconnect/reconnect only.**

The external SGT2271 monitor returned with the same QueryDisplayConfig device path and source/target identity on the same connection.

This is not sufficient to freeze a durable display key. Reboot, primary-role change, port swap and identical-panel ambiguity remain relevant.

## Remaining physical protocol
