## Change

Added `app/src/tracking/evidence_sampler.h` with a value-owned `EvidenceSnapshot` and one `EvidenceSampler`.

The sampler now owns the current Win32 and UIA providers and produces one snapshot per render-loop tick containing:

- merged source-relevant candidates
- sample timestamp
- last pointer-movement timestamp
- UIA snapshot timestamp
- current UIA caret/focus presence flags

`CaptureRunner` now consumes that snapshot.

Deliberately unchanged in Slice 2:

- follow eligibility filtering remains after sampling
- ViewController arbitration remains unchanged
- locator policy remains unchanged
- capture/presentation ownership remains unchanged
- no view-ownership policy was introduced

The sampler header also defines `NOMINMAX` before Windows-dependent provider headers so it is independently compilable.
