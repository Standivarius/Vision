**Date:** 2026-09-30

## Evidence on MARIUS-DELL
Current Windows PnP/WMI evidence:
- Integrated panel: PnP `DISPLAY\BOE07BB\...UID265988`; manufacturer BOE; product 07BB; EDID serial reported as `0`.
- HDMI panel: PnP `DISPLAY\SGT2271\...UID198171`; manufacturer SGT; product 2271; EDID serial reported as `0`.

Therefore EDID serial is not a sufficient durable display identity on the actual development hardware.

PnP instance paths distinguish the current endpoints, but their connection-instance suffixes are not yet proven stable across port swap/reboot. Geometry and `\\.\DISPLAYn` are runtime resolution evidence, not durable identity truth.

A read-only QueryDisplayConfig correlation probe was added under `lab/automation-harness/display_identity_probe.ps1`, but execution of its native interop was blocked by the local bridge safety layer. No attempt was made to bypass that boundary.

Reboot, unplug/replug, port swap and primary-display mutation were deliberately not performed because development is required to avoid unnecessary owner interruption.

## Slice 6 structural result
Added:
- `MonitorIdentityHints`
- `MonitorSnapshot`
- `TopologySnapshot` with monotonic generation
- `MonitorRegistry` with canonical ordering and runtime topology change detection
- `WorkspaceIntent` separate from `ResolvedWorkspace`

Runtime monitor enumeration now feeds bounds, primary state, effective DPI when available, orientation and current GDI device-name hint into MonitorRegistry. WorkspaceCoordinator receives the current topology generation.

Most importantly, startup no longer rewrites persisted Context/Detail/Reference intent merely because the current topology resolves differently or falls back to one monitor. Explicit user settings remain the path that changes intent.

## Durable-key recommendation
Do not freeze a single durable display key yet. Preserve a composite identity-hints structure and resolution strategy capable of later incorporating QueryDisplayConfig adapter/target/path information plus PnP/EDID metadata. Treat geometry and GDI display number as current-topology properties.

This is sufficient to proceed with in-process topology reconciliation without pretending display identity is solved.
