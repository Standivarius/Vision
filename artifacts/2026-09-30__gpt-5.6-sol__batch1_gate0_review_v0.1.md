## Gate 0B — geometry / magnification contract

Added:

- `app/src/core/geometry_contract.h`
- `app/tests/geometry_contract_tests.cpp`
- `docs/project/foundation/geometry_magnification_contract.md`

The pure contract defines:

- physical source/destination pixels as the render geometry basis;
- requested source viewport = destination size / zoom;
- uniform X/Y scale;
- explicit clamp and letterbox/pillarbox when source cannot fill destination at requested zoom;
- 1x as pixel-scale semantics, distinct from overview/fit;
- explicit negative-origin and source-resize handling;
- DPI conversion as an API-boundary concern, not part of physical magnification semantics.

Covered cases include matched 1920x1080 at 1x/2x/4x, larger-to-smaller, smaller-to-larger, portrait destination, partial destination, negative virtual origins, physical-DPI invariance, edge clamp, source resize and overview-vs-1x distinction.

The geometry contract is **not wired into runtime yet**, so current qualified behaviour remains unchanged.
