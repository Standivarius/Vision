## Harness changes made while establishing the suite

New reusable runners:

- `app\run-two-screen-acceptance.ps1`
- `app\run-ui-surface-smoke.ps1`

Existing smokes were hardened where acceptance evidence exposed weaknesses:

- browser/UI automation was made deterministic around real Vision window ownership rather than assuming the visible Detail HWND owns global command handling;
- telemetry analysis in hotkey/return/integrated smokes now considers numeric frame rows only, excluding `# event=...` lifecycle markers;
- integrated mouse/viewport smoke now fails unless locator-visible frames exist, viewport decisions are valid and Present failures are zero;
- the acceptance runner backs up the real `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Standivarius\Visual\settings.ini`, restores it in `finally`, and verifies byte-equivalent restoration by SHA-256.

## Important boundary

VIS-005 is a repeatable two-screen software acceptance suite, not a replacement for:

- destructive clean-install/reinstall lifecycle verification;
- Authenticode/signing qualification;
- SM768/SM770 hardware qualification;
- user/assessor decisions such as source-to-Detail pointer-boundary UX;
- pixel-level colour-transform calibration.

Those remain separate programme items/gates.
