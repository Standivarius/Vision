## Excel

Canonical run:
lab/benchmarks/integrated_app/runs/2026-10-01_06-57-30__excel_editing_smoke

Tested:
- disposable unsaved workbook;
- cell-grid navigation;
- formula-bar editing;
- return to grid.

Observed:
- active cell: ControlType.DataItem / XLSpreadsheetCell;
- cell patterns include Value, GridItem, SelectionItem and TextPattern;
- formula bar: ControlType.Edit / XLFormulaBarEditor with TextPattern;
- grid tracked-target ratio 98.7%;
- 26 distinct tracked cell rectangles;
- grid locator visible 148/150 frames;
- formula caret present 88.7%;
- formula semantic caret selected 97%;
- formula locator visible 129/133 frames;
- return-to-grid tracked frames: 57;
- zero Present failures;
- no forced Vision cleanup;
- workbook closed without saving.

Important interpretation:
Excel grid navigation does not primarily appear as UIA focus selection in Vision telemetry. It is correctly tracked through the Win32 caret route while UIA focus remains current. The changing selected rectangles prove the tracked target moves with cell navigation. A focus-only acceptance rule would therefore be wrong for Excel.

Verdict:
Bounded-good for ordinary cell navigation and formula-bar editing on this Office/Windows/topology baseline.

## Boundary

These results do not certify:
- Word add-ins, complex dialogs, comments/review panes, tables/charts or every document mode;
- Excel charts, Power Query, modal dialogs, add-ins or complex custom panes;
- Outlook or Teams;
- browser rich editors.

They are task-level current-version compatibility evidence, not application-family blanket guarantees.
