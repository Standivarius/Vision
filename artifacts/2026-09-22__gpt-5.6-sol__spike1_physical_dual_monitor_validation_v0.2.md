## Timing caveat

`Direct3D11CaptureFrame.SystemRelativeTime` remains a raw compositor QPC timestamp. Signed differences between it and callback/render QPC are diagnostic only and are not interpreted as photon latency, frame age or user-visible end-to-end latency.

## Coordinate/topology observation

The PowerShell environment collector recorded:

- primary display bounds: 1280x720 at x=0;
- external display bounds: 1920x1080 at x=1920.

This indicates that the collector and/or involved APIs are exposing coordinate spaces affected by DPI scaling rather than one simple contiguous physical-pixel space. This is not a failure of the cross-monitor renderer, but it is strong concrete motivation for the next coordinate-truth spike.

## Conclusion

The 60-second physical-console run materially strengthens the WGC + D3D11 hypothesis:

- two physical displays were active;
- the full deterministic scenario completed;
- the Detail view operated across monitors;
- no Present failures occurred;
- no GPU-copy fallback was needed;
- no large inter-arrival stall comparable to the earlier 169 ms outlier appeared;
- the user observed successful behavior throughout the run.

This is sufficient evidence to stop treating basic WGC + D3D11 cross-monitor viability as the primary open question.

Spike 1 should remain available for targeted follow-up work—recording correlation, repeated performance characterization, hot-plug/sleep-wake recovery, mixed-DPI stress and later Doxa/four-display validation—but most development effort should now move to the next independent capability spikes.
